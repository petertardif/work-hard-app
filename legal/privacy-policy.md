# Privacy Policy — Work Hard

**Last updated: 14 October 2026**

Work Hard is a personal accountability app. This policy describes what it collects,
why, where it is kept, and how to get rid of it.

## What is collected

**Your email address.** Used to create your account and sign you in. Nothing else.

**What you put into the app:**

- Lists you create, and the tasks in them
- Runs — the start date, how many days you chose, and whether each one is still
  going, finished or over
- Which tasks you completed, and on which day
- Quotes you add to your quote library
- Your settings: time zone, the hour your day ends, your reminder time, which mark
  appears beside a task, and whether the daily quote is shown

**On your device only:** a cached copy of the above so the app works without a
signal, and the date the daily quote was last shown. This never leaves your phone
except as the ordinary syncing of the data already listed.

## What is not collected

No analytics. No advertising identifiers. No tracking of any kind, across this app or
any other. No location. No contacts. No health data. No device fingerprinting. No
third-party SDKs that gather behaviour.

Notifications are **local**: reminders are scheduled by the app on your own phone. No
push token is ever generated or sent anywhere, and no server knows when or whether you
were reminded.

## Where it is kept

In a [Supabase](https://supabase.com) project — a hosted PostgreSQL database — owned
by the developer of this app. Database access rules restrict every row to the account
that created it, so one user cannot read another's data.

Traffic between the app and the database is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.

## Who it is shared with

Nobody. Your data is not sold, rented, traded, or handed to advertisers, data brokers
or analytics companies.

The only third party involved is Supabase, which hosts the database on the developer's
behalf and processes data solely to provide that hosting.

Data may be disclosed if required by law, or to investigate suspected abuse of the
service.

## How long it is kept

Until you delete it. Delete a list, a quote or an account and the corresponding rows
are removed.

## Deleting your account

**Settings → Delete account**, inside the app.

This is permanent and immediate. It removes your login, your profile and settings,
every list and task, your entire run history and completion record, and your whole
quote library. There is no soft delete, no recovery window, and no retained copy.

## Children

Work Hard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their
information.

## Your rights

You can see everything the app holds about you from inside it, export your run history
from **Settings → Export data**, correct anything by editing it, and delete all of it
from Settings. For anything else, contact the developer at the address below.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or
the CCPA, including the right to request a copy of your data or to object to its
processing. The in-app tools above satisfy most such requests immediately.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and the updated
