## What the app does

Work Hard tracks a set of daily tasks across a run of consecutive days. Complete every
task each day and the run continues. Miss one task on one day and the run ends, and
your next attempt starts again at day one.

This is a self-reported tracker. It takes your word for what you have done and verifies
nothing.
